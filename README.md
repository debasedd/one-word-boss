A tiny program for the [One Word for the Boss](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-badger-44-one-word-for-the-boss-6b48c735) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

It writes one word to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly one word
describing my boss:

> Hovercraft

He does not walk past your desk, he hovers over it — silent,
floating just behind your shoulder, watching the screen until you
feel the draught. True in any kitchen, warehouse or office.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
